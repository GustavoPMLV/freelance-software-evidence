How the check decides

Written rules. No guesses about your file.

Reading a CSV in the browser is only the start. The value is in the rules: which key identifies a line, how prices are compared and what happens when something repeats. Every rule is explicit and shows up in the report.

  1. Text keys

    Order + SKU are read as text. Leading zeros and letter case count.

    0042 ≠ 42

  2. Exact cents

    Whole-number quantities and prices in cents, with no rounding. An equal total cannot hide a different field.

    BRL 19.90 → 1990 cents

  3. Repeats block

    A repeated key or invalid row is never summed or overwritten, and the batch is not reported as issue-free.

    2 × same key → blocked

  4. Source line

    Every issue points to the physical line in both files, even after blank lines or fields with line breaks.

    line 4 in A · missing in B

Before we talk

Direct questions, bounded answers.

Does it already work with my ERP?

The demonstration compares the reference format described above. Your layout needs to be reviewed in the pilot. No integration, certification or partnership with specific ERPs is claimed.

Why not use a spreadsheet and VLOOKUP?

For a one-off check, that may be enough. The pilot delivers written rules for your format: composite key, exact cents, repeats blocked instead of summed, and a line-level report someone else can re-check.

Do my files leave my computer?

Not in the demonstration. Reading and checking happen in this browser, with no upload, analytics or persistent storage. To evaluate a pilot, share only sanitized samples, with no credentials or personal data.

Does a no-differences result approve the import?

No. It reports only the rules that were checked. Tax, inventory, master-data and ERP rules may require further checks by the person responsible.

How does the pilot avoid becoming unlimited support?

The proposal records layouts, rules, acceptance criteria and the limit of one revision round. Format changes, integrations and new workflows receive a separate quote.

Is anyone using it already?

Not yet. This is a working demonstration with no validated buyers. The first pilots exist to confirm whether the problem repeats and whether the configuration is worth the price.
